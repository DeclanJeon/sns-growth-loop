# Baseline observations — no skill loaded

Three direct coaching prompts were answered without `sns-growth-loop`.

| Scenario | Observed baseline | Gap the skill must close |
|---|---|---|
| Starting after months of overthinking | Recommended publishing immediately, a 30-day cadence, and one learning goal per post. | Add the source's deliberate-practice framing (100 attempts as practice, not a reach guarantee) and a repeatable per-post feedback record. |
| Weak early retention | Correctly diagnosed the title/cover-to-intro mismatch and proposed a single-variable experiment against the creator's baseline. | Make the source-specific sequence explicit: demonstrate the title/thumbnail promise in the first five seconds, then give only necessary context; label cited 30-second benchmarks as video-specific hypotheses, not universal platform standards. |
| Solo creator with limited time | Recommended sustainable cadence, analytics, focused feedback, collaboration, and no forced daily posting. | Operationalize the two feedback windows (capture issues while creating; review analytics and rewatch later), category-based review, and a structured peer data-sharing/mastermind loop. |

The baseline was already competent; the skill's purpose is consistent, source-traceable application of those distinct steps and careful handling of platform-specific metrics—not claiming generic advice is unique or guaranteeing growth.
